and one picture is another project's own. Each keeps the terms of its source.

2. media/port-qt-treadmill.png, media/port-qt-treadmill-sporty.png and media/originals/qt-treadmill-original.png
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

media/originals/qt-treadmill-original.png is a picture of the original, unchanged: the file
examples/dataImports/DataModels/images/treadmill_demo.png of the repository
https://git.qt.io/public-demos/qtdesign-studio, commit 0c1ccf18 of 2026-05-08. It shows the
Sporty look of the example TreadmillMcuDemo.

sizes, pictures (the wordmark, the slogan and the icons) and behaviour are taken from

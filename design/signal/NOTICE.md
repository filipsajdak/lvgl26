# Signal

The design system of the lvgl26 device-family showcase: see `README.md` for its rules and
`tokens.design.json` for its tokens, in the list format of the design tool it is kept in.
lvgl26 reads design tokens in the W3C Design Tokens format; the converter is not written yet.

are under the SIL Open Font Licence 1.1 (`site/media/fonts/OFL.txt`).

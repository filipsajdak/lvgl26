Coffee machine, redesigned in Signal

A 480 × 320 touch panel on Signal's day theme. Tap through the live panel, review every screen, then read what the review found and what changed.

Live panel

Flow: Drink → Your cup → Brewing → Enjoy. The gear opens Service.

One pattern on every screen: the title is always top left, the step is always top right, going back is always bottom left, going on is always bottom right.

The drink photos were generated for this mock-up and cut out, so they sit on any tile colour. Swipe is proposed with the framework's gesture feature.

What the review found, and what changed

PracticeProblem in the last versionNow
ConsistencyThe title jumped right when a back arrow appeared; the price was "2.20 €" on tiles and "2.20 EUR" in the summary.Title fixed top left. Back and Cancel live bottom left, the forward action bottom right. One price format everywhere.
Recognition over recallAbstract cup glyphs looked alike; step dots had no number.A photo of each drink on its tile, in the summary and on the done screen. Dots carry "2 / 4".
Visible state, not colour aloneThe selected tile differed only by colour. Strength showed bars with no value.The selected tile also gets a tick. Strength says "4 of 5 · Strong"; size shows millilitres between its buttons.
Alignment and rhythmThe two steppers had different widths, so the + buttons did not line up. The summary panel touched the Brew button.Both steppers share one grid: −, value, +. A token gap separates content from the footer on every screen.
AffordanceThe back arrow was a small character. Service rows gave no hint of what they do. Tiles had lost their surface colour.Back is a 48 px labelled button. Each service row ends in its control: a chevron, − and +, or a switch. Tiles sit on the surface colour again.
Feedback and error preventionBrewing showed only a number. Enjoy did not say what happens next. A stepper could go below its range.Brewing names its stage. Enjoy says when it returns to the drinks. − and + dim at the ends of the range.
Touch targetsFine before.Every control is still at least 48 × 48.

Motion, and the lvgl26 feature each one shows

WhereWhat movesTokensBuilt with

DrinksThe tick pops on the chosen tilemotion-enter, ease-backA task that waits for the click, then animates a scale
BrewingThe ring fills, the numeral counts down, the stage name changes: grinding, extracting, finishingThe brew time of the drinkOne task: an animation of the ring's value, a loop that waits a second, and cancel as "whichever comes first"
EnjoyThe cup settles with a small overshoot; three steam wisps rise and fade in a loop; the mark pops; the name slides upmotion-settle 480 ms, ease-back; motion-steam 5.2 sTasks owned by the page: they start when it is shown and end when it leaves

The steam is one small picture used three times and tinted from a token, so it works on both themes. The motion durations and curves are proposed design tokens. Whether a picture's opacity and position can both be animated today still has to be checked in the library.
